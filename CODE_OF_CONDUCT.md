# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Criticise code, not the person who wrote it. Assume the
other person is competent and acting in good faith until you have real evidence
otherwise — and if you do have it, report it rather than litigating in a thread.

## The standard

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1][cc]. In short, these are
not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other project space:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments based on who someone is.

## Technical disagreement is not a conduct problem

Rux is opinionated and its design laws rule things out on purpose. Arguing
forcefully that a decision is wrong — with reasons — is welcome and is how the
design improves. What is not welcome is making it personal, repeating a rejected
argument indefinitely, or treating a maintainer's "no" as an insult.

A decision you disagree with is not misconduct. Being rude about it is.

## Reporting

Report to **ainedixon01@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept
confidential; you will get a response. If your report concerns the maintainer,
you may instead use GitHub's [report abuse][abuse] flow, which does not route
through this project.

Consequences scale with severity and range from a private word to a permanent
ban from project spaces.

[cc]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
[abuse]: https://github.com/contact/report-abuse
